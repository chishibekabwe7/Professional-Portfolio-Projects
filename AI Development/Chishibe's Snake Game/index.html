How to Play

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD steer. Space or P pauses. R restarts. M mutes. Esc opens the menu. Swipe on the board or use the touch pad.

Food and power-ups

Normal food grows by one. Golden food is worth five and expires. Slow-down, score multiplier, and shrink power-ups change the run.
