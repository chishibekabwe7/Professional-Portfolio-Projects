About the Library

Supporting teaching, learning and research at Copperbelt University

Our mission

A trusted gateway to knowledge

Copperbelt University Library provides equitable access to reliable information, quiet study spaces and professional support for teaching, learning and research. CULMS will make it easier for every member to find and manage library resources.

Our services are coordinated across three locations so that students and staff can use the library wherever they study or teach.
